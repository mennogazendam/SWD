Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a salt water disposal (SWD) facility?

A salt water disposal facility is the surface infrastructure — storage, pumps, gathering lines, and injection wellhead equipment — that safely receives and disposes of produced water from oil and gas operations, typically by injecting it into a permitted disposal well.

Who regulates SWD facilities in Texas?

SWD and injection wells in Texas fall under state and federal regulatory oversight specific to the oil and gas industry. EPCM supports clients through the applicable permitting and compliance process as part of the build.

Does EPCM handle permitting for SWD facilities?

Yes — EPCM provides permitting support alongside the design and construction work, so the facility and the regulatory process move together rather than one waiting on the other.

What types of injection wells does EPCM build facilities for?

EPCM builds and upgrades facilities for disposal wells, commercial disposal facilities, industrial injection wells, municipal injection wells, and geothermal wells.

Can EPCM upgrade or expand an existing SWD facility instead of building new?

Yes — EPCM takes on refurbishment, upgrade, and optimization work on existing facilities as readily as new turnkey construction, using the same team throughout.
